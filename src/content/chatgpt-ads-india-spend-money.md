# ChatGPT Ads Are Live in India. Should You Actually Spend Money on Them?

OpenAI switched on ChatGPT Ads for Indian users on 27 August 2026, and the self-serve Ads Manager opened to Indian advertisers on 4 September. Billing is in rupees, the minimum daily budget is ₹725, and there's no invitation process or spend commitment.

## What ChatGPT Ads Actually Are

They're sponsored cards that appear below a ChatGPT answer when the system judges your offer relevant to the conversation someone is having. The structural difference from Google Ads matters more than the format: **you don't bid on keywords.**

## Who Actually Sees These Ads

Ads appear for people on ChatGPT's Free and Go plans. Paid tiers — Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Edu — carry no ads at all.

## What the Real Test Data Shows

SE Ranking published a detailed account of their own ChatGPT Ads campaign. Their test reported 97,131 impressions, 1,263 clicks and a 1.30% CTR, with a blended CPC of $3.16, but almost no sign-ups.

## What This Costs in Practice

The minimum daily budget in India is ₹725 per campaign. A daily budget is an average across a seven-day period rather than a hard daily cap.

## Who Has a Genuine Case for Testing This Now

E-commerce, D2C, consumer products, travel, education and businesses with a short measurable conversion path have clearer reasons to test the format.

## One Thing Worth Separating Clearly

Advertising on ChatGPT does not make ChatGPT recommend you. Paid placement and organic AI visibility are separate workstreams.

## My Actual Recommendation

For e-commerce and D2C brands with working conversion tracking, a small controlled test can provide useful learning. For many other Indian businesses, especially narrow local services, fixing existing channels may be the more measurable next step.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are ChatGPT Ads available in India?

Yes. Ads went live for Indian users on 27 August 2026, with self-serve access for Indian advertisers from 4 September 2026.

### What is the minimum budget for ChatGPT Ads in India?

₹725 per day per campaign.

### Can I target keywords on ChatGPT Ads?

No. Advertisers use context hints rather than keyword bidding.

### Will advertising on ChatGPT get my brand mentioned in its answers?

No. Paid placement does not guarantee organic citations in ChatGPT answers.

### Should a small local business in India try ChatGPT Ads?

Generally not yet. Narrow local conversation volume may be thin and delivery can be unpredictable.
